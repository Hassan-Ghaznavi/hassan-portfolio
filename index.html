The person behind the pencil

A developer.
An artist.
Always a player.

I'm Hassan Ghaznavi, a game developer and 3D environment artist based in Karachi, Pakistan. I study Computer Science & Information Technology at NED University, specializing in Gaming & Animation.

My work spans gameplay systems, gesture tracking, and interactive 3D environments. I enjoy taking an idea from its first sketch to something you can actually play.

As Game Development & Animation Domain Lead at Gameverse Society, I also mentor junior developers and help teams turn their ideas into games.
